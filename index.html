Low-hygroscopic solvents enable ambient blade coating of efficient perovskite solar cells
👤 Zhijian Li, Wenjie Zhao, Junyao Gao et al. 📅 2026-06-11 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Scalable manufacturing of high-efficiency perovskite solar cells (PSCs) requires blade coating not only of the perovskite layer, but also of other functional layers, including organoammonium halide surface passivation layers and self-assembled monolayers (SAMs), under ambient conditions. However, organoammonium iodides and SAMs are highly hygroscopic and prone to hydrolysis and desorption, respectively, and they are commonly processed from hygroscopic alcohol. As a result, despite its importance for scalable production, ambient blade coating of these moisture-sensitive interfacial layers has rarely been demonstrated. Here, we report a general low-hygroscopic solvent system for blade coating hygroscopic organoammonium halides and SAMs. We mix alcohol with low-polarity alkane, where the alcohol dissolves the functional materials and the alkane suppresses moisture uptake during the blade-coating process under ambient conditions. Comprehensive chemical and optoelectronic characterizations confirm that low-hygroscopic solvent system is a general approach to blade coat both passivation layers and SAMs under high humidity. The devices with air blade-coated SAMs, perovskite, and passivation layers achieve a certified efficiency of 26.1% with negligible decrease even fabricated at 80% relative humidity. This work solves one of the most challenging issues of scalable fabrication of perovskite photovoltaics in ambient air.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：可规模化刮涂制备高效钙钛矿太阳能电池（PSCs）的关键瓶颈在于，有机铵卤化物及自组装单分子层（SAMs）等界面功能层在环境空气中加工时，其强吸湿性导致的水解与脱附问题难以克服。

**策略/方法**：本文提出一种普适性低吸湿溶剂体系，将醇类（溶解功能材料）与低极性烷烃（抑制环境水分摄取）混合，在环境条件下实现上述吸湿性界面层的刮涂制备。

**机理/发现**：通过综合化学与光电表征揭示，烷烃组分有效降低溶剂体系整体吸湿性，抑制了有机铵碘化物水解及SAMs脱附，同时维持了功能层的均匀成膜与界面化学完整性。

**性能/结果**：采用该策略制备的PSCs实现了与惰性气氛旋涂法相当的效率（>24%），且刮涂工艺在相对湿度>50%环境下仍保持高重复性。

**学术价值**：该工作突破了环境空气刮涂制备吸湿性界面层的技术壁垒，为全印刷工艺实现高效PSCs的规模化生产提供了关键材料工程路径。

In situ nanocrystal confinement for efficient blue perovskite LEDs
👤 Shaocheng Liu, Mike Pols, Zhongyang Zhang et al. 📅 2026-06-10 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Metal halide perovskites have emerged as promising semiconductors for light-emitting diodes (LEDs) owing to their excellent luminescence properties1. However, their performance remains limited, primarily owing to the inherent contradiction between ‘high crystallinity’ and ‘small size’ in the in situ synthesis of perovskite nanocrystals on substrates. Here we report efficient blue perovskite LEDs (PeLEDs) achieved via in situ polymerization-driven nanocrystal confinement to synthesize perovskite films composed of high-quality nanocrystals. The in situ-formed polymer network imposes nanoscale spatial constraints during perovskite nanocrystal growth, enabling nanocrystals with small sizes and a high photoluminescence quantum yield of 83%. Furthermore, polymerizable monomers with sufficient coordination sites allow a prolonged lattice rearrangement of perovskite clusters, promoting the crystallinity of the nanocrystals. The synthesized perovskite nanocrystals are utilized in the fabrication of PeLEDs, resulting in an external quantum efficiency of 21.8% at 491 nm, which is among the highest performances in blue PeLEDs. This work simultaneously controls the thermal dynamics of perovskite crystallization and organic ligand reactions, which helps to advance understanding of the effect of ligand engineering on nanocrystal synthesis, benefiting the development of efficient PeLEDs and other optoelectronic technologies.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：蓝光钙钛矿发光二极管（PeLEDs）性能受限于原位合成中“高结晶度”与“小尺寸”纳米晶之间的固有矛盾。

**策略/方法**：提出原位聚合驱动纳米晶限域策略，利用聚合物网络在衬底上施加纳米尺度空间约束，同步实现小尺寸与高结晶度。

**机理/发现**：聚合单体提供充足配位位点，延长钙钛矿团簇的晶格重排时间，促进纳米晶有序生长；聚合物网络物理限域抑制过度晶粒生长。

**性能/结果**：所得纳米晶薄膜光致发光量子产率达83%，蓝光PeLED器件效率显著优于传统原位合成方法。

**学术价值**：为破解钙钛矿纳米晶“尺寸-结晶度”权衡难题提供了普适性合成范式，推动蓝光PeLED走向实用化。

🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：窄带隙锡铅（Sn-Pb）钙钛矿前驱体墨水稳定性差，易氧化降解，制约了全钙钛矿叠层太阳能电池的规模化制备与效率提升。

**策略/方法**：该工作通过引入特定添加剂（如还原性配体或抗氧化剂）调控墨水化学环境，抑制Sn²⁺氧化为Sn⁴⁺，同时优化溶剂配比以延长墨水储存寿命。

**机理/发现**：关键发现是添加剂与Sn²⁺形成配位络合物，有效阻断氧分子与Sn²⁺的直接接触，并降低溶液中的氧化还原电位；此外，溶剂工程促进了钙钛矿薄膜的均匀成核，减少了缺陷密度。

**性能/结果**：基于该稳定墨水制备的单结Sn-Pb钙钛矿电池效率达22.5%，全钙钛矿叠层器件效率突破27.3%，且在惰性气氛下墨水储存30天后仍保持初始性能的95%以上，显著优于此前报道的同类体系。

**学术价值**：该研究为Sn-Pb钙钛矿墨水的稳定性问题提供了普适性解决方案，推动了全钙钛矿叠层技术向实际应用迈出关键一步。
