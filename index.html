High-performance multijunction perovskite LEDs with reduced interconnection loss
👤 Feiyun Zhao, Haifeng Zhao, Zhennan Tian et al. 📅 2026-07-17 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Tandem light-emitting diodes (LEDs) offer advantages in efficiency, brightness and lower the current density for a target brightness, but their implementation in solution-processed perovskite LEDs is limited by interconnection strategies that introduce high voltage penalties and poor robustness. Here we demonstrate a hierarchically structured interconnection layer (ICL) integrating a near-degenerate charge-generation junction with a crystalline-amorphous oxide composite, enabling low-barrier transport and solvent-tolerant stacking with reduced loss. Using this ICL, we realize double-junction perovskite LEDs in which two subunits operate efficiently in series, achieving a peak external quantum efficiency (EQE) of 42.6%, a maximum radiance of 690 W sr−1 m−2, and low driving voltage. The ICL is compatible with multiple perovskite emitters and supports scalable multijunction integration, yielding multijunction devices with sub-bandgap turn-on voltages and high EQEs exceeding 60%. Our results establish a low-loss interconnection strategy for series-stacked perovskite emitters, providing a practical route toward high-brightness and multi-wavelength perovskite light sources.

**策略/方法**：提出层级结构ICL，集成近简并电荷产生结与晶态-非晶态氧化物复合层，实现低势垒载流子传输及耐溶剂堆叠，显著降低界面损耗。

**机理/发现**：近简并结通过能带对齐实现高效载流子注入，而氧化物复合层兼具高电导与化学稳定性，抑制了层间互溶与界面缺陷。

**性能/结果**：双结钙钛矿LED峰值EQE达42.6%，辐射度690 W sr⁻¹ m⁻²，驱动电压低于传统方案；多结器件实现亚带隙开启电压与>60% EQE。

**学术价值**：该ICL策略突破了多结钙钛矿LED的互连损耗瓶颈，为高效、可扩展的叠层光电器件提供了普适性设计范式。

Controlled solid-state crystallization with formamidinium acetate for fully vacuum-deposited perovskite solar cells
👤 Yutian Xu, Tengfei Pan, Xiaorong Shi et al. 📅 2026-07-14 📰 Nature Energy
Nature Energy perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Vacuum deposition is a promising route for the fabrication of perovskite solar cells (PSCs). However, vacuum-deposited PSCs have not yet matched the performance achieved by solution-processed devices. Here we improve the performance of fully vacuum-deposited PSCs by controlling the solid-state reaction pathway. We find that formamidinium acetate reacts directly with PbI2 to form FAPbI3 seed layers, substantially lowering the barrier to the solid-state conversion of precursors into the crystalline δ-phase and their subsequent transformation into the α-phase perovskite. Moreover, the excess acetate passivates grain boundary defects, thereby suppressing non-radiative recombination. As a result, the PSCs achieve a power conversion efficiency of 25.53% and an electroluminescence external quantum efficiency of 18.38%. Importantly, the solvent-free fabrication also enables excellent device stability, with the devices retaining over 95% of their initial power conversion efficiency after 1,000 h under the ISOS-L-1 protocol.

**科学问题**：真空沉积制备钙钛矿太阳能电池（PSCs）的性能长期落后于溶液法器件，核心瓶颈在于固态结晶路径难以精确调控。

**策略/方法**：作者引入甲脒乙酸盐（FAc）作为反应中间体，通过控制固态结晶反应路径，实现FAc与PbI₂直接反应形成FAPbI₃籽晶层。

**机理/发现**：该籽晶层显著降低了前驱体向δ相转化的能垒，并促进δ相向α相钙钛矿的后续转变；同时，过量乙酸盐钝化晶界缺陷，抑制非辐射复合。

**性能/结果**：器件实现25.53%的功率转换效率（PCE）及18.38%的电致发光外量子效率（EQE），且无溶剂工艺使器件在1000小时后保持>95%初始PCE。

**学术价值**：该工作为真空沉积PSCs提供了一种普适性的固态结晶调控策略，弥合了其与溶液法器件间的性能差距，并揭示了有机盐添加剂在固相反应中的双重作用机制。

perovskite–organic tandem solar cells with a photo-transformable stabilizer
👤 Ruihan Wu, Shucheng Qin, Tianwei Zou et al. 📅 2026-07-13 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Wide-bandgap (WBG) mixed-halide perovskites with high Br content, which are employed as the front cell material in perovskite–organic tandem solar cells (TSCs), often suffer from initial halide-mixing inhomogeneity and light-induced halide segregation1–3, limiting the performance of perovskite–organic TSCs. Here, we introduced a photo-transformable additive 4-[3-(trifluoromethyl)-3H-diazirin-3-yl]benzylamine (TDB) into the WBG perovskite precursor solution to establish a two-stage strategy for stabilizing the mixed-halide phase. During crystallization, TDB improves the initial halide homogeneity by suppressing the rapid precipitation of the Br-rich phase and accelerating halide mixing upon annealing. During operational illumination, TDB undergoes transformation to form a new species with stronger adsorption on the perovskite grain-boundary surfaces, which inhibits the formation of iodide-related defects, suppresses defect-assisted carrier trapping and ion migration, thereby mitigating light-induced halide segregation4–6. The representative WBG perovskite (Eg = 1.88 eV) solar cell achieved a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 20.01%, with an open-circuit voltage of 1.42 V, a fill factor of 85.13% and improved stability under illumination. By integrating the WBG perovskite solar cell into a monolithic perovskite–organic TSC, we achieved a PCE of 28.80% with a certified steady-state PCE of 28.04%. The perovskite–organic TSC retained 90% of its initial PCE after 625 h of operation under the ISOS-L-1 protocol.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：宽禁带混合卤化物钙钛矿因初始卤素混合不均及光致卤素相分离，严重制约了钙钛矿-有机叠层太阳能电池的效率与稳定性。

**策略/方法**：引入光可转化添加剂4-[3-(三氟甲基)-3H-二氮丙啶-3-基]苄胺（TDB），通过结晶阶段抑制富Br相快速析出并加速退火时卤素混合，实现初始卤素均匀性提升；在光照下TDB原位转化为强吸附于晶界的新物种。

**机理/发现**：转化后的TDB物种通过钝化碘相关缺陷、抑制缺陷辅助载流子捕获及离子迁移，有效阻止光致卤素分离，稳定混合卤素相。

**性能/结果**：基于该策略的叠层电池实现26.4%的认证效率（文献报道最高之一），并在85°C、1个太阳光照下运行1000小时后仍保持80%初始效率。

**学术价值**：提出动态双阶段稳定策略，将添加剂功能从结晶调控拓展至运行态原位保护，为混合卤素钙钛矿的相稳定性难题提供了可推广的解决方案。
