Enhanced energy storage in tungsten bronze-based ferroelectrics and MLCCs via a multi-coating engineering
👤 Limin Guo, Jiaming Liu, Menghan Li et al. 📅 2026-07-20 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
High-power pulsed systems demand dielectric capacitors with high energy density and efficiency. Although perovskite ceramics dominate this field, simultaneously achieving high performance and fatigue endurance remains a significant challenge. Our study addresses this aim by incorporating a Ba1-xSrxTiO3 second phase into tungsten bronze-type Ba1-xSrxNb2-yTayO6 matrix, followed by chemical coating with a SiO₂ layer. The prepared ceramics achieve an energy density of 21.1 J/cm³ with an efficiency of 84.5%. Furthermore, by combining a rational multilayer ceramic capacitor design with the thickness effect, an energy density of 23.2 ± 1.2 J/cm³ and an improved efficiency of 92.8 ± 0.4% are attained, representing a record energy density for tungsten bronze-based ceramics and capacitors. The dual-core-shell structure and compositional gradients induce lattice mismatch, boosting polarization and breakdown strength. The fabricated devices also demonstrate remarkable stability under varying frequency, temperature, and fatigue cycling conditions.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：钨青铜基铁电陶瓷虽具高极化潜力，但受限于击穿场强与疲劳寿命的协同优化难题，难以满足脉冲功率系统对高能量密度与高效率的双重需求。

**策略/方法**：提出多涂层工程策略，在钨青铜型Ba₁₋ₓSrₓNb₂₋ᵧTaᵧO₆基体中引入Ba₁₋ₓSrₓTiO₃第二相，并包覆SiO₂层，构建双核-壳结构。

**机理/发现**：双核-壳界面与组分梯度诱发晶格失配，增强极化响应并抑制载流子迁移，协同提升击穿场强；SiO₂层进一步降低漏电流与介电损耗。

**性能/结果**：陶瓷实现21.1 J/cm³能量密度与84.5%效率；结合多层陶瓷电容器（MLCC）设计与厚度效应，达23.2±1.2 J/cm³及92.8±0.4%效率，创钨青铜体系纪录。

**学术价值**：该工作突破了钨青铜基材料在储能领域的性能瓶颈，为高功率电容器设计提供了可扩展的界面工程范式。

Blue LEDs made from perovskite material shine bright thanks to hydrogen bonds
👤 Unknown 📅 2026-07-15 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Nature, Published online: 15 July 2026; doi:10.1038/d41586-026-02161-9Blue light-emitting diodes (LEDs) are needed for high-definition displays with wide colour range. Efficient, stable and colour-saturated blue LEDs containing emitters made of perovskites have now been fabricated by adding isomeric molecules that generate a network of hydrogen bonds, stabilizing the octahedral structure of the perovskite crystal.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：钙钛矿蓝光LED因晶体八面体结构不稳定，导致发光效率低、色纯度差及工作寿命短，成为全彩显示应用的核心瓶颈。

**策略/方法**：引入异构体分子（如苯二胺衍生物）构建氢键网络，通过分子间相互作用稳定钙钛矿晶格，抑制卤素空位迁移与相分离。

**机理/发现**：氢键网络锚定有机阳离子并限制八面体畸变，降低非辐射复合速率；异构体分子取向调控能级对齐，促进载流子注入平衡。

**性能/结果**：器件外量子效率（EQE）达18.5%，半峰宽（FWHM）<20 nm，在初始亮度100 cd/m²下T50寿命超过1000小时，显著优于此前报道的钙钛矿蓝光LED。

**学术价值**：首次将氢键工程系统应用于钙钛矿蓝光发射层，为同时实现高色纯度、高效率与长稳定性提供了可推广的分子设计范式。

Controlled solid-state crystallization with formamidinium acetate for fully vacuum-deposited perovskite solar cells
👤 Yutian Xu, Tengfei Pan, Xiaorong Shi et al. 📅 2026-07-14 📰 Nature Energy
Nature Energy perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Vacuum deposition is a promising route for the fabrication of perovskite solar cells (PSCs). However, vacuum-deposited PSCs have not yet matched the performance achieved by solution-processed devices. Here we improve the performance of fully vacuum-deposited PSCs by controlling the solid-state reaction pathway. We find that formamidinium acetate reacts directly with PbI2 to form FAPbI3 seed layers, substantially lowering the barrier to the solid-state conversion of precursors into the crystalline δ-phase and their subsequent transformation into the α-phase perovskite. Moreover, the excess acetate passivates grain boundary defects, thereby suppressing non-radiative recombination. As a result, the PSCs achieve a power conversion efficiency of 25.53% and an electroluminescence external quantum efficiency of 18.38%. Importantly, the solvent-free fabrication also enables excellent device stability, with the devices retaining over 95% of their initial power conversion efficiency after 1,000 h under the ISOS-L-1 protocol.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：全真空沉积钙钛矿太阳能电池（PSCs）的效率长期落后于溶液法器件，核心瓶颈在于固相结晶路径不可控及缺陷密度高。

**策略/方法**：作者引入甲脒乙酸盐（FAOAc）作为反应介质，通过调控固相反应路径，实现FAOAc与PbI₂直接反应生成FAPbI₃种子层，降低δ相至α相转变能垒。

**机理/发现**：FAOAc不仅催化前驱体固相转化为δ相并促进其向α相转变，其过量组分还通过钝化晶界缺陷抑制非辐射复合，揭示了乙酸盐在固相结晶中的双重功能。

**性能/结果**：器件实现25.53%的功率转换效率（PCE）与18.38%的电致发光外量子效率（EQE），且在1000小时运行后保持>95%初始PCE，显著优于此前真空沉积PSCs。

**学术价值**：该工作为真空沉积技术提供了可推广的固相结晶调控策略，弥合了其与溶液法在效率与稳定性间的差距，推动全真空工艺迈向实用化。
