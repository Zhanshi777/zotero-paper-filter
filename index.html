📝 原文摘要
Existing encapsulation cannot prevent oxygen ingress in perovskite solar modules, leading to rapid degradation. We show that oxidation of C60, rather than the perovskite, causes the failure of solar cells with C60 as the electron transport layer. Replacing C60 with strain-relieved C70, together with a dense ALD-SnO2 buffer, suppresses oxygen reactivity and diffusion, respectively.
