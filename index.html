Addressing the 1000-h/1000-nit stability challenge in perovskite LEDs
👤 Yucai Yuan, Shiyu Xing, Gan Zhang et al. 📅 2026-11-06 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Perovskite light-emitting diodes (PeLEDs) offer exceptional colour purity and efficiency for next-generation display applications. Despite the recent advances in device stability, achieving long-term stable PeLEDs under display-relevant conditions remains a critical challenge. The actual device half-life (T50) experimentally measured for visible PeLEDs were in most cases limited to below 1000 h for any given brightness, in contrast to the much longer extrapolated lifetimes reported. Here, we demonstrate stable green PeLEDs with a T50 lifetime of 1350 h at an initial luminance of 1000 cd m−2 (nits), a maximum energy-conversion efficiency of 28.0%, and a peak external quantum efficiency of 24.5%. The exceptional performance is enabled by the insertion of a fluorinated self-assembled monolayer between the metal oxide and polymeric hole-transport layers. This molecular interlayer leads to Ohmic-like characteristics for charge injection, lowering the operating voltage at ~1000 nits to sub-bandgap levels (~2.1 V) where ion migration is suppressed. The accumulated photon output of our PeLEDs over the course of their T50 lifespan is found to sharply increase as the driving voltage drops to sub-bandgap values, highlighting the critical role of employing low-voltage device architectures in reaching the 1350-h/1000-nit stability milestone. Finally, a flexible active-matrix PeLED display prototype is developed, showing the potential of the devices for wearable optoelectronics.

作者在金属氧化物与聚合物空穴传输层间引入氟化自组装单分子层作为分子界面层，实现类欧姆电荷注入并降低工作电压。

其价值在于确立了以实测工况寿命为基准的评价范式，并提供可推广的界面工程策略。

Conjugation-regulated self-assembled molecules for tandem solar cells
👤 Zheng Fang, Zhijie Rao, Chongtao Liu et al. 📅 2026-11-04 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Perovskite/silicon tandem solar cells on rough textured substrates are at present limited by a critical trade-off in self-assembled monolayer (SAM) design because increasing molecular conjugation enhances conductivity but often leads to unfavourable aggregation that impairs conformal coverage1,2. Here we overcome this challenge using a rationally engineered molecular spatial conjugation that incorporates tailored intramolecular distortion to suppress excess π–π stacking without sacrificing charge-transport efficiency. This steric regulation facilitates the formation of a highly ordered, conformal SAM across rough pyramidal textures. By reconciling molecular packing with interfacial coverage, the tailored interface provides optimized energy-level alignment for ultrafast charge extraction, enables improved defect passivation through adaptive conformational adjustment and acts as a robust template for high-quality perovskite film growth. Making use of this interface, the optimized tandem device achieves a notable fill factor (FF) of 85%, yielding a certified stabilized power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 35.1%. Furthermore, the device demonstrates outstanding stability, retaining more than 94% of its initial performance after 1,500 h of ageing under the ISOS-L-1 protocol. Our findings provide a clear molecular engineering route to reconcile conductivity and film uniformity while maximizing the performance of tandem photovoltaics on textured silicon substrates.
