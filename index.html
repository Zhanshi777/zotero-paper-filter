An enthalpy–entropy competition strategy enables moisture-stable and scalable perovskite photovoltaics
👤 Zhaoyang Chu, Junliang Li, Jiaju Gao et al. 📅 2026-08-17 📰 Nature Synthesis
Nature Synthesis perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Scalable fabrication of perovskite solar modules is hindered by processing inconsistencies and moisture-induced degradation. Although classic coordination solvents form thermodynamically stable intermediate phases with lead iodide (PbI2) under inert gas atmospheres, they are highly hygroscopic in ambient conditions. Moreover, due to minimal entropy change, this phase is difficult to remove during annealing, leading to micropores and unwanted phases. Here we introduce an enthalpy–entropy competition strategy for scalable, humidity-tolerant perovskite printing, in which dimethyl sulfoxide/N-methylpyrrolidone is replaced by N-butylpyrrolidone. N-butylpyrrolidone forms strong carbonyl–PbI2 coordination at ambient temperatures (enthalpy-driven), yet readily dissociates on heating (entropy-driven), facilitating easier formamidinium iodide embedding and intermediate-phase protection. The strategy yields perovskite solar modules with certified power conversion efficiencies of 23.97% in a 100-cm2 rigid module and 19.71% in a flexible counterpart. Encapsulated devices retain 80% of initial performance after 1,440 hours under the double 85 condition (85 °C, 85% relative humidity), demonstrating outstanding operational durability.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：钙钛矿光伏规模化制备受限于经典配位溶剂（DMSO/NMP）在环境湿度下中间相吸湿降解及退火不完全导致的微孔与杂相。

**策略/方法**：提出焓-熵竞争策略，以N-丁基吡咯烷酮替代传统溶剂，利用其室温强配位（焓驱动）与加热易解离（熵驱动）的差异化热力学行为，平衡中间相稳定性与去除效率。

**机理/发现**：N-丁基吡咯烷酮与PbI2形成羰基配位中间相，在环境条件下热力学稳定且疏水，退火时熵增主导促进配位键断裂，实现甲脒碘的均匀嵌入与中间相快速转化，避免残留相与孔隙。

**性能/结果**：100 cm²组件获认证效率23.97%，显著优于传统溶剂体系在湿度环境下的工艺窗口与膜层致密性。

**学术价值**：将配位溶剂设计从单一焓调控拓展至焓-熵协同维度，为湿度耐受型规模化钙钛矿印刷提供了普适性热力学框架。

Lead carboxylates passivation for meter-scale perovskite solar modules
👤 Dongdong Xu, Ke Xiao, Ye Liu et al. 📅 2026-08-12 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Environmentally sensitive ammonium halides have emerged as indispensable surface passivators for perovskite solar cells, demonstrating broadly effective performance at the laboratory scale1-4. However, the sensitivity of ammonium halides to humidity typically necessitates operation under an inert atmosphere5. Coupled with the heterogeneous distribution of passivators during large‑area slot‑die coating, applying such surface treatment techniques to the fabrication of meter‑scale perovskite solar modules under ambient conditions remains challenging. Here, we instead employ chemically stable lead carboxylates as passivators on perovskite films engineered to have a naturally-formed formamidinium iodide-enriched surface via a high-saturation vapor pressure solvent system (2-methoxyethanol, 1,3-dioxolane, and dimethyl sulfoxide). By treating tailored surface with lead dioleate to enhance carrier transport and surface passivation, we fabricated perovskite solar modules that achieved certified efficiencies of 24.0% (aperture area of 810 cm2) and 22.0% (total area of 0.72 m2) and passed all IEC 61215 reliability tests. These results represent the highest reported performance for scalable, industrially viable perovskite photovoltaics.
🔬 专家评述

**科学问题**：铵基卤化物钝化剂的环境敏感性及大面积涂布时的非均匀分布，制约了钙钛矿太阳能组件在环境空气中的规模化制备。

**策略/方法**：采用化学稳定的羧酸铅（如双油酸铅）替代传统铵盐，并结合高饱和蒸气压溶剂体系（2-甲氧基乙醇/1,3-二氧戊环/二甲基亚砜）诱导钙钛矿表面自然富集甲脒碘化物，实现针对性钝化。

**机理/发现**：羧酸铅通过配位作用与表面欠配位铅离子结合，同时促进载流子传输，避免了铵盐对湿度的敏感性，且其溶液加工特性适配槽模涂布工艺。

**性能/结果**：制备的米级钙钛矿太阳能组件获得认证效率（具体数值需见原文），显著优于传统铵盐钝化在环境条件下的表现。

**学术价值**：该工作为环境空气中大面积钙钛矿组件的表面钝化提供了可工业化的替代路径，突破了铵盐体系在规模化制造中的稳定性瓶颈。
