Hydrogel-confined environment for aqueous-compatible perovskite crystallization
👤 Aiping Zhang, Huiyi Zong, Ruihan Yuan et al. 📅 2026-10-16 📰 Nature Synthesis
Nature Synthesis perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Halide perovskite synthesis generally uses toxic, high-boiling-point and strongly coordinating organic solvents, hindering scalable processing and crystallographic control in thick-film applications. Water is ideal as a solvent but incompatible with perovskite synthesis due to precursor insolubility and rapid hydrolysis in water. Here we introduce a hydrogel-confined reaction environment that overcomes this incompatibility by decoupling precursor solvation from lattice formation. Within a polymer–water network, PbI2 and organic iodides are dispersed in a stabilized ionic state that suppresses aggregation and kinetically inhibits water-induced decomposition. Concurrently, this network anchors emerging nuclei and imposes spatial confinement, directing crystal growth into dense thick films. Spectroscopic and computational analysis reveals facet-specific coordination between polymer and perovskite, suppressing surface defect formation and preserving efficient charge transport despite the presence of the polymer matrix. The resultant thick hydrogel-grown films (180 µm) exhibit charge carrier mobilities of ∼10 cm2 V−1 s−1. When directly integrated into pixelated X-ray imaging sensors, the device achieves a high sensitivity-to-noise ratio of \(25,000\,\upmu {\text{C}}\,{{\text{Gy}}}_{{\text{air}}}^{-1}\,{{\text{cm}}}^{-2}\,{{\text{pA}}}^{-1}\), with an ultralow detection limit of 19.8 nGyair s−1, enabling high-fidelity imaging with negligible signal crosstalk.

作者提出水凝胶限域反应环境，将前驱体溶剂化与晶格形成解耦，使PbI₂与有机碘盐在聚合物-水网络中稳定分散为离子态，抑制聚集并动力学阻断水解。

Hidden reaction pathways inform additive engineering for perovskite LEDs
👤 Sung-Doo Baek, Yu-Ting Yang, Pengfei Wu et al. 📅 2026-10-14 📰 Nature
Nature perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Perovskite light-emitting diodes (PeLEDs) have emerged as a promising class of solution-processed light-emitting devices, combining high efficiency with facile fabrication1,2,3,4,5. Amino-acid-based molecular additives are widely used to regulate crystallization and suppress defects in perovskite thin films6,7,8, yet these molecules are chemically reactive in precursor solutions, in which their transformations and products remain poorly understood. Here we resolve and control the hidden solution-phase chemistry of a ubiquitous additive, 5-aminovaleric acid (5AVA), whose solution-phase reactivity has remained unclear6,9,10,11,12,13,14,15. We show that 5AVA undergoes competing in situ reactions during precursor preconditioning, yielding two chemically and functionally distinct products: a beneficial organoamidinium zwitterion and a detrimental Lewis-base lactam. By explaining this reaction dichotomy and establishing targeted reaction-pathway control, we selectively use the beneficial species. Direct synthesis and incorporation of the purified zwitterion decouple its intrinsic passivation function from confounding by-products, yielding perovskite films with a near-unity mean peak photoluminescence quantum yield (PLQY) of 97.2% and PeLEDs with a champion external quantum efficiency (EQE) of 37.1%, alongside a mean peak EQE of 32.8%. Finally, we demonstrate that this reaction-pathway control concept can be extended to further additive chemistries and emission colours, highlighting its potential as a chemistry-driven strategy for rational additive design in PeLEDs.
🔬 专家评述

该工作针对氨基酸类添加剂在钙钛矿前驱液中化学反应性不明、产物-功能关联缺失这一瓶颈。

作者以5-氨基戊酸（5AVA）为模型，结合原位表征解析其前驱体预处理阶段的竞争反应路径，并建立靶向调控策略，实现有益产物选择性富集。

关键发现是5AVA原位生成两类功能迥异产物：有益的有机脒鎓两性离子与有害的Lewis碱性内酰胺，二者分别主导缺陷钝化与发光猝灭。

该研究揭示了添加剂“隐藏反应”这一被长期忽视的维度，为添加剂工程提供了反应路径层面的设计原则。
