Joule perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Efficient perovskite photovoltaics require durable interfaces compatible with scalable manufacturing. Solution-processable metal-oxo clusters combine robust inorganic frameworks with tunable organic components to enhance charge extraction and suppress ion migration. Complementary clusters stabilize both interfaces of the electron-transport layer, enabling a certified efficiency of 26.7% and improved operational stability. This modular strategy links molecular interface engineering with scalable coating, offering a pathway toward efficient, durable perovskite solar technologies.
