👤 Minhuan Wang, Yepin Zhao, Zhenyang Suo et al. 📅 2026-10-31 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
The morphology of the initial PbI2 film is very pivotal in dictating the uniformity and crystallinity of the resultant perovskite film in the two-step sequential deposition processes. A solution-based induction is introduced to transform PbI2 clusters into PbI2 nanowires to achieve controllable morphology of the PbI2 film. The nanowires act as scaffolds for the subsequent perovskite formation. An in-plane low-angle diffraction detected in the in-situ grazing-incidence small-angle X-ray scattering (GISAXS) for the PbI2 film indicates the formation of a periodically arranged ordered structure. The PbI2 nanowires facilitate the ion-exchange process, markedly improving the homogeneity and orientation of the perovskite films, which is confirmed by the in-situ grazing-incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering (GIWAXS). As a result, a champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.94% (certified as 26.66%, a very high certification efficiency of n-i-p structured perovskite devices) is achieved for the laboratory area (aperture area of the mask is 0.0618 cm2). Moreover, a micro module device with an aperture area of 21.54 cm2 was successfully manufactured and achieved a PCE of 24.60% (certified as 24.02%).

perovskite lattice compliance enables efficient and stable bifacial perovskite/silicon tandem photovoltaics
👤 Fuzong Xu, Jiang Liu, Xuechun Zhang et al. 📅 2026-10-29 📰 Nature Energy
Nature Energy perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Bifacial perovskite/silicon tandem solar cells offer a promising route to high area-normalized power output. Using ~1.55 eV perovskite absorbers reduces reliance on bromide, largely mitigating light-induced halide segregation and shifting the dominant stability challenge to the intrinsic photothermal resilience of bromide-lean absorbers. Here we identify perovskite lattice compliance, the ability of the crystal lattice to elastically accommodate stress, as a materials descriptor governing photothermal stability. Across the compositions studied, higher lattice compliance promotes photothermal stability by suppressing thermally induced microstrain accumulation, shifting deep-level defects towards shallower states and reducing band-edge energetic disorder. Guided by this absorber-level design principle, bifacial monolithic perovskite/silicon tandems achieved a power-generation density of 36.2 mW cm−2 under 0.20 albedo-equivalent illumination and retained 90% of their initial efficiency after over 1,000 h of ISOS-L-2 testing (1-sun at 65 °C). These results establish lattice compliance as a physically testable framework for designing stable perovskite absorbers.

其创新在于将弹性应力容纳能力与微应变累积、深能级缺陷深度及带边能量无序度建立关联，揭示顺应性提升通过抑制热致微应变累积、促使缺陷能级浅化来改善稳定性。
