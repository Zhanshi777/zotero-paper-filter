Wide-bandgap perovskite with homogeneous crystallization and suppressed halogen segregation for efficient perovskite-organic tandem solar cells
👤 Xinhong Zhu, Yuwei Duan, Zhuang Xie et al. 📅 2026-10-01 📰 Nature Communications
Nature Communications perovskite
📝 原文摘要
The differences in crystallization and migration barriers of iodide and bromide within wide-bandgap perovskite result in inhomogeneous crystallization and halogen-phase segregation, limiting photovoltaic device performance. Here we report a sequential regulation engineering to synchronously homogenize crystallization and stabilize crystallographic phase by adding 2-iminothiolane (ITA). ITA priorly undergoes an in situ thiolation reaction with methylamine to yield a reductive 4-mercapto-N-methylformamidine (MMF). It effectively modulates perovskite crystallization toward homogenization, scavenges the detrimental molecular iodine or iodine radicals by redox reaction, and anchors the migratory iodide ions by hydrogen-bond interaction. Leveraging these contributions, the obtained perovskite films exhibit the suppressed halogen phase and enhanced ion migration barrier along the vacancy and interstitial defects-depending migration paths, achieving an efficiency of 20.45%. Moreover, the corresponding champion tandem solar cell (TSC) achieves an efficiency of 28.20% (certified 27.82%), retaining 90% initial efficiency by continuous tracking maximum power point after 700 h under ISOS-L-1 protocol.

作者引入2-亚氨基硫杂环戊烷（ITA），其与甲胺原位硫醇化生成还原性MMF，同步实现结晶均一化、碘自由基清除及碘离子氢键锚定。

Geometric frustration in morphologically chiral nanoribbons of layered perovskites
👤 Wenhao Shao, Zhichen Nian, Yuan Lu et al. 📅 2026-09-29 📰 Nature Synthesis
Nature Synthesis perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Twisting and chiral growth are unusual in crystals and rarely observed in layered materials. Here we show that layered achiral metal-halide perovskites spontaneously form twisted and helical ribbons. Microscopy, diffraction and molecular dynamics simulations reveal that hydrogen-bonded organic networks within the lattice generate mismatched strains, causing ribbons to bend and twist when the strain is released, similar to the chiral opening of seed pods. This mismatch, known as geometric frustration, originates from crystal symmetry and can be tuned through molecular doping. Controlling growth kinetics twists nearly all ribbons towards one direction, giving bulk samples chiroptical activity. Statistical and mechanical analyses show that ribbon shape is governed by intrinsic elastic strain and geometric constraints; modulating them enables a continuous transition from twisted to helical ribbons. Our results show how molecular-scale interactions can generate macroscopic chiral shapes, providing a route to control chiral morphologies in layered two-dimensional materials.
🔬 专家评述

该研究针对层状无手性金属卤化物钙钛矿中自发产生扭转与螺旋形貌这一反常晶体生长瓶颈，通过氢键有机网络引入晶格应变失配，结合分子掺杂与生长动力学调控实现手性形貌构筑。

显微、衍射与分子动力学模拟揭示，几何阻挫源于晶体对称性导致的应变不匹配，释放时驱动带体弯曲与扭转，机制类似豆荚手性开裂。

调控弹性应变与几何约束可实现从扭转带到螺旋带的连续转变，并赋予块体样品手性光学活性。
