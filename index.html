Grain boundary engineering using metallomesogen in formamidinium–caesium lead triiodide for stable perovskite/silicon tandem solar cells
👤 Biao Li, Xingtao Wang, Yehui Wen et al. 📅 2026-11-02 📰 Nature Energy
Nature Energy perovskite
📝 原文摘要
Formamidinium (FA)-doped caesium lead triiodide, Cs(FA)PbI3, is a promising front-cell absorber for tandem solar cells owing to its good stability, yet obtaining high-quality films remains a challenge. Here we identify that sparse doping of oversized FA cations induces severe grain boundary (GB) distortion, thereby facilitating defect generation and performance deterioration. To mitigate this, we develop a Pb-based metallomesogen that forms a mesophase exhibiting both solid-like and liquid-like properties. The phase characteristics enable the Pb-based metallomesogen to distribute uniformly along GBs of Cs(FA)PbI3 film, alleviating GB distortion, relieving shear strain, preserving lattice integrity and mitigating defect formation. Consequently, perovskite/silicon tandem solar cells based on a 1.66-eV Cs(FA)PbI3 absorber incorporating a Br-containing GB modifier achieve a champion efficiency of 32.08% (certified 31.62%), retaining 95% of the initial performance after ~3,000 h under maximum power point tracking and 93% after 1,500 h under 85% relative humidity and 85 °C damp-heat stressing.
🔬 专家评述

该研究针对FA掺杂CsPbI₃前驱体薄膜中稀疏掺入的大尺寸FA阳离子诱发晶界畸变、加剧缺陷生成并劣化器件性能这一瓶颈。

作者设计Pb基金属介晶，利用其兼具类固/类液特性的介相在晶界均匀铺展，缓解剪切应变、保持晶格完整性并抑制缺陷。

含Br晶界修饰的1.66 eV吸收层实现32.08%（认证31.62%）的钙钛矿/硅叠层效率，约3000 h后保持初始性能95%。

该工作将金属介晶引入晶界工程，为宽禁带钙钛矿的应变管理与稳定性协同优化提供了新范式。

📝 原文摘要

Here we present an artificial intelligence-guided design framework for self-assembled monolayers that integrates graph neural network-based retrosynthetic predictions with high-throughput screening based on density functional theory and expert chemical knowledge. Over 90,000 structures from 308 fragments were narrowed down to 300 candidates, ranked using a composite score of eight descriptors extending beyond intrinsic electronic properties to include synthesizability, hole reorganization energy λh and structural features (heteroatom content and aromaticity). The top candidate, AI-1, is readily synthesizable and incorporates favourable heteroatom functionalities. AI-1 achieves optimal energetic alignment and forms robust S–Pb coordination bonds at the buried interface, effectively suppressing non-radiative recombination and enhancing interfacial stability. Devices incorporating AI-1 achieved a power-conversion efficiency of 27.2% and retained >95% of their initial efficiency after 2,000 h of maximum-power-point tracking at 65 °C. Mechanistic studies show that the multifunctional methylthio side chain is vital, as it reinforces the interfacial bonding and balances passivation with chemical robustness. These findings demonstrate that artificial intelligence-guided design can systematically deliver self-assembled monolayers uniting high efficiency with long-term operational stability, and they provide general principles for interface engineering.
